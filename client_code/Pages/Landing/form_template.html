From a hum to a finished song

  1. Pick a style

    Eighteen styles start you with a real arrangement: drums, bass, chords and a hook, in the right tempo and key, with intro, verses, choruses and the transitions between them.

  2. Sing, hum or beatbox

    Record your voice over the song with a count-in and the words scrolling by. Hum a melody and it becomes notes. Beatbox and it becomes a drum part.

  3. Shape it in the ribbon

    Home, Insert, Record, Voice, Instrument, Effects, Song, Lyrics, Mix: tabs you already know how to use. Then mix it down to a WAV or a MIDI file.

A voice menu, not a voice generator

You sing; Humbeat makes it sound like the record you hear in your head. Every setting runs on your own device, on your own voice.

Auto-tune
Snaps your pitch to the song's key. Retune speed goes from natural to the instant robotic snap of trap and mumble rap.
Harmony and choir
A second voice a third or fifth above you, or a three-voice choir, always in the key.
Character
Chipmunk to giant: move the voice's character without changing the note you sang.
Double, reverb, echo, grit
Stacked pop vocals, a stadium room, a hopecore haze, a metal scream, an old telephone.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Making, recording, exporting and sharing songs is free. There are no ads.

Do I need to know music?

No. A style gives you a finished-sounding song to start from; you change one thing at a time. The guide explains tracks, chords, effects and song structure in plain words.

Can I make something like Michael Jackson, Britney Spears or Taylor Swift?

You can make songs in those styles: 80s dance pop, Y2K teen pop, storyteller pop. Humbeat gives you the ingredients (drums, bass, chords, vocal sound, structure); the song, the melody and the voice are yours. Humbeat doesn't copy anyone's recordings or voice.

Does it make up vocals for me?

No, and on purpose: you sing or hum, and the voice menu shapes it. Nothing is generated.

Where do my recordings go?

As a visitor, only into this browser. With an account, into your songs on our server, so you can open them anywhere and share them. You can delete them any time.

What can I take away?

A WAV file of the whole mix, and a MIDI file with every instrument part for any other music app.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in any modern browser, though a laptop with headphones is the most comfortable for recording.
